# CrazyAuto 4.4 rear modeling references

These links were used as shape and component references. No photos, meshes, or textures were copied into the game. Check the exact model year and trim before further sculpting; the result is a stylized interpretation.

| Car | Reference | Rear cues used |
|---|---|---|
| Evo X (2008) | [Mitsubishi 2008 Evolution brochure](https://autocatalogarchive.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Mitsubishi-Lancer-Evolution-2008-USA.pdf) | Clear reflector areas within the rear lamp clusters; distinct sedan trunk and wing |
| STI Hawkeye (2006) | [Subaru Canada 2006 Impreza brochure](https://www.subaru.ca/content/7907/media/en-ca/download/06ImpBrochure.pdf) | Dual-element WRC box-type spoiler with integrated brake light; swept outer clusters |
| Evo III (1995) | [Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution III brochure archive](https://autocatalogarchive.com/mitsubishi/) and [Mitsubishi Evolution buyers guide](https://manuals.plus/m/e5840a14b420b67c0af4cfc1c2098d6d656887ebf409db713ee3e2b2dfd8b94d.pdf) | Rectangular rear clusters and taller wing with high-mounted brake lamp |
| Sport Quattro S1 E2 | [Audi Tradition S1 E2 collection photo](https://www.audi.com/en/photos/detail/audi-tradition-s-book-insights-114186) and [Audi Sport Quattro S1 Pikes Peak photo](https://www.audi.com/en/photos/detail/audi-sport-quattro-s1-pikes-peak-19277) | Square tail units and extreme rally aero |
| RS 6 Avant C8 | [Audi MediaCenter RS 6 Avant information](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/15878/file/b1d88632d3d98a1d5e5a281460e22fadd7233567/en_BasisInfo_RS_6_Avant.pdf?1572966423=&disposition=attachment) | Separate segmented rear lamps, roof edge spoiler, rear diffuser, twin oval outlets |

Modeling rule: check direct rear, rear three-quarter, and side views at the same camera scale. Confirm the lamp housing is seated against the curved body shell, exhaust openings are round or oval in actual geometry, and wing supports join the trunk rather than floating. Keep each car's rear signature separate in code and preserve previous releases.
